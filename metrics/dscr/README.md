# Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

DSCR measures how many times annual net operating income covers scheduled annual debt service.

## Formula

```text
DSCR = Annual NOI / Annual Debt Service
```

## Inputs and conventions

- NOI is calculated before financing costs.
- Debt service includes required principal and interest for the measured period.
- If multiple loans exist, specify whether the ratio covers senior debt only or total property debt.
- Interest-only, amortizing, floating-rate, and stressed debt service can produce materially different results.

## Interpretation

`1.00x` means NOI exactly equals scheduled debt service. Above `1.00x` indicates a cushion; below `1.00x` indicates property operations alone do not cover the scheduled payment. Lenders often impose minimum DSCR requirements, but the applicable threshold depends on asset and loan terms.

## Worked example

With NOI of `$600,000` and annual principal and interest of `$450,000`:

```text
$600,000 / $450,000 = 1.33x
```

## Limitations and checks

- DSCR does not directly measure loan balance or collateral value.
- It may omit capital expenditures, tenant improvements, leasing commissions, and replacement reserves unless the stated convention includes them.
- Stress-test vacancy, expenses, rate caps, refinancing, and amortization changes. Review debt yield and LTV alongside DSCR.
