# Net Operating Income (NOI)

NOI is property operating income remaining after vacancy, credit loss, and ordinary operating expenses, but before debt service, depreciation, income taxes, and owner-specific financing costs.

## Formula

```text
Effective Gross Income = Potential Gross Income - Vacancy/Credit Loss + Other Income
NOI = Effective Gross Income - Operating Expenses
```

## Inputs and conventions

- Income may include rent and recurring property-level ancillary income.
- Operating expenses commonly include management, repairs, utilities paid by ownership, insurance, and property taxes.
- Debt service, depreciation, income tax, acquisition costs, and sale proceeds are excluded.
- Capital expenditures and replacement reserves must be labeled because industry treatment varies.

## Interpretation

NOI is the core unlevered earnings measure used in cap rate, DSCR, and debt yield. Growth in NOI can result from rent growth, occupancy improvement, other income, or expense control; the source matters.

## Worked example

Potential rent of `$900,000`, vacancy of `$45,000`, other income of `$25,000`, and expenses of `$330,000` produce:

```text
$900,000 - $45,000 + $25,000 - $330,000 = $550,000 NOI
```

## Limitations and checks

- NOI can be distorted by omitted expenses, temporary occupancy, or nonrecurring income.
- Compare trailing, current, budgeted, and stabilized versions with a clear reconciliation.
- Review rent roll, operating statements, expense benchmarks, and capital needs.
