# Occupancy Break-even

Occupancy break-even estimates the share of potential gross income required to cover operating expenses and scheduled debt service.

## Formula

```text
Occupancy Break-even = (Operating Expenses + Debt Service) / Potential Gross Income
```

## Inputs and conventions

- Potential gross income assumes full occupancy at the modeled rents before vacancy and collection loss.
- Operating expenses should use the same annual period and exclude debt service.
- Debt service includes scheduled principal and interest under the modeled loan terms.
- For mixed-use assets, use a consistent rentable-base and income convention.

## Interpretation

An `82%` result means the property needs approximately 82% of its potential gross income to cover the included expenses and debt service. A lower break-even level generally indicates more operating cushion.

## Worked example

With potential gross income of `$1,000,000`, expenses of `$360,000`, and debt service of `$440,000`:

```text
($360,000 + $440,000) / $1,000,000 = 80.0%
```

## Limitations and checks

- Occupancy and collections are not identical; concessions and bad debt may reduce income at a stated physical occupancy.
- Large capital items may be excluded from operating expenses.
- Compare the result with actual and market occupancy, lease rollover, DSCR, expense volatility, and reserve needs.
