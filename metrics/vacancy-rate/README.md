# Vacancy Rate

Vacancy rate measures the unused share of rentable inventory or potential rental income during a stated period.

## Formulas

Physical vacancy:

```text
Vacancy Rate = Vacant Units / Total Rentable Units
```

Economic vacancy:

```text
Economic Vacancy = Lost Rental Income / Potential Gross Rental Income
```

## Inputs and conventions

- Physical vacancy counts units or area; economic vacancy measures lost income.
- State the observation date or averaging period.
- Distinguish vacant-ready, down, model, employee, and preleased units where relevant.
- Concessions, bad debt, and collection loss may be reported separately or included in economic loss.

## Interpretation

Physical and economic vacancy answer different questions. A property can be highly occupied but suffer meaningful economic loss from concessions or delinquency. Compare actual vacancy with stabilized underwriting and relevant market evidence.

## Worked example

If 6 of 100 rentable units are vacant:

```text
6 / 100 = 6.0% physical vacancy
```

## Limitations and checks

- A point-in-time count may hide turnover or seasonality.
- Unit-based vacancy can misrepresent mixed unit sizes or commercial area.
- Review rent roll status, collections, lease expirations, market vacancy, and loss-to-lease.
